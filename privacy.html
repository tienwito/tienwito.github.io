Privacy Policy – rclone-fc27

Last updated: 4 October 2026

rclone-fc27 is a private tool used only by its owner, Tienwito Karnthanat, to upload video files he created from his own computer to his own Google Drive.

Data accessed

The app accesses only the owner's Google Drive to upload and list files in a folder he chooses. It does not access other users' data.

Data use and sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
